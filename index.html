Board Certified in Clinical Neuropsychology

She is dedicated to providing the highest standard of care to her patients and is committed to helping them achieve their full potential.

Dr. Perumparaichallai completed her internship and postdoctoral residency in clinical neuropsychology at Barrow Neurological Institute (BNI), St Joseph's Hospital and Medical Center, Phoenix, AZ. She is a licensed psychologist in Arizona from 2011.

Dr. Perumparaichallai's expertise includes:

Conducting neuropsychological evaluations for adolescents, adults, and seniors with various neurological, neurosurgical, neurodegenerative, neurodevelopmental, and neuropsychiatric conditions.
Helping patients identify their cognitive strengths, develop effective strategies to manage challenges, and establish realistic and sustainable life goals.
Utilizing evidence-based treatment methods supported by empirical research.

Dr. Perumparaichallai is an accomplished researcher specializing in epilepsy, acquired brain injury, and neurorehabilitation outcome. She has actively participated as a distinguished speaker at both national and international conferences focused on neuropsychology and neurorehabilitation. She has published several articles published in peer-reviewed journals.

We specialize in conducting comprehensive neuropsychological evaluations to enhance our understanding of patients' cognitive and emotional functioning. Our assessments are available for individuals ranging from adolescents (14 years and above) to adults and seniors, addressing a diverse range of referral questions.

Examples of common referral issues:

  • Traumatic Brain Injury: What are the enduring effects of an injury and what treatment might help?
  • Memory and Aging: Is this normal age-related change or a disease? Or is it something else?
  • Learning and Development: Does this patient have a developmental disorder affecting learning? If so, how can we help to circumvent these weaknesses and provide the best learning environment for success?
  • Changes in Personality and Behavior: Are these symptoms of a psychiatric disorder or do they signify a brain-related syndrome?

We are dedicated to empowering individuals to leverage their strengths, overcome challenges, and improve their quality of life. Our primary goal is to assist you in identifying and utilizing your unique abilities while providing guidance on how to navigate and overcome cognitive obstacles. By developing and implementing effective cognitive compensatory strategies, we aim to enhance your independence and overall well-being.

Psychotherapy aims to help individuals capitalize on their strengths and compensate for difficulties related to neuropsychological conditions. We offer psychotherapy work with patients and families to:

  • Build insight into their current capabilities
  • Strengthen coping
  • Enhance adaptation to life changes
  • Provide emotional support through the experience of neurological conditions and recovery
  • Addressing social skills needs
  • Improving interpersonal relationships/support

We achieve this by personalizing a treatment plan that meets the needs of our patients and their families.

We provide personalized cognitive rehabilitation as part of our comprehensive treatment services. Our aim is to assist you in progressing in your brain recovery journey or adapting to a medical condition that affects your functioning. Cognitive rehabilitation entails a targeted approach of therapeutic cognitive activities, designed to address and improve the individual's brain-behavior deficits, guided by a thorough assessment and understanding of their unique needs.

Our goal is to help you move forward to where you want to be in your life. Services are directed to achieve functional changes by:

  • Reinforcing, strengthening, or reestablishing previously learned patterns of behavior, or
  • Establishing new patterns of cognitive activity or compensatory mechanisms for impaired neurological systems

Welcome to our Memory Rehabilitation Clinic, where we specialize in providing expert services for individuals of all ages — adolescents, adults, and seniors — who are facing memory challenges. Our clinic offers tailored solutions for a wide range of neurological and medical conditions that can affect memory function.

Memory problems can stem from many possible causes, including anxiety, mood issues, medical issues, vitamin deficiency, medication side effects, and neurological or neurosurgical disorders. Some of the memory problems are reversible.

Our Memory Rehabilitation clinic services help with:

  • Identifying the primary and secondary causes of memory issues through a comprehensive neuropsychological evaluation
  • Helping you understand the nature of your memory problems
  • Developing suitable cognitive compensatory strategies that are suitable for your lifestyle and situations
  • Training to use the memory strategies in clinic and real-life situations to enhance your independence and confidence at home, school, work, or other settings
  • Caregiver education to understand their loved one's memory issues and training to support them to utilize compensatory strategies in their daily life

Clinical Neuropsychology

What is Clinical Neuropsychology

Clinical neuropsychology is a specialized field within clinical psychology that focuses on understanding the intricate connections between the brain and behavior. It delves into the diagnosis of brain disorders, evaluation of cognitive and behavioral functioning, and the development of effective treatments.

A clinical neuropsychologist is a highly trained, independent professional with a doctoral level of expertise. They offer comprehensive assessment and intervention services to individuals of all ages, utilizing the principles and scientific concepts of clinical neuropsychology.

What happens during a neuropsychological evaluation?

The neuropsychological evaluation usually takes 4 to 6 hours for the following procedures:

1

Gathering Historical Information

History is obtained through reviewing medical and other records, and through interview with the patient. With the patient's permission, family members or other knowledgeable persons may be interviewed.

2

Neuropsychological Testing

The examination typically consists of the administration of standardized tests using oral questions, paper and pencil, computers, the manipulation of materials such as blocks and puzzles, and other procedures.

3

Analysis & Integration

The doctor will analyze the performance by comparing with appropriate normative data and integrate this within the context of the clinical history, patient presentation, and referral question. A written report is produced.

4

Feedback

A feedback session is provided to review the findings and recommendations with the patient and family. With your permission, the report can be shared with the referring doctor and other health care providers.

How do I know if my loved one needs a Neuropsychological Evaluation?

An acquired brain injury from a trauma to the head, stroke, lack of oxygen, or an infection
A medical or neurological condition such as hydrocephalus, cerebral palsy, epilepsy (seizures), neurofibromatosis, tuberous sclerosis, or a brain tumor
Neuropsychiatric disorders
Neurodegenerative disorders
A developmental or school problem such as a learning disability, attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder, neurogenetic disorder, neurodegenerative disorder

Your physician may recommend a neuropsychological assessment to:

  • Help make or confirm a diagnosis
  • Get a record of your loved one's functioning before treatment with medicine or surgery
  • Record a change in your loved one's functioning after a medical treatment (testing can be repeated to see if the treatment changed your child's functioning)

How does a Neuropsychological Evaluation differ from a school evaluation?

Neuropsychologists and school psychologists often use some of the same tests. However, school evaluations focus on deciding if a child has a problem with academic skills such as reading, spelling, or math. Neuropsychologists focus on understanding why a child is having problems in school or at home. This is done by examining academic skills but also examining all of the thinking skills needed to perform well in and outside of school — skills like memory, attention, and problem-solving. Understanding a child's specific thinking strengths and weaknesses helps to better focus school plans and medical treatment and understand potential areas of future difficulty. Because neuropsychologists have training in clinical psychology, they are also able to diagnosis emotional problems like depression and anxiety.
